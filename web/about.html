About the game

Aryan Arora and Aarush Sahai made System Failure for the Haven Pune GameJam. It’s a Pygame arcade shooter about flying a ship that keeps breaking in new ways.

Learn to fly it again

One round can reverse your controls. The next can jam your weapon or send your ship drifting. All nine malfunctions change how you survive the next wave.

You start with five health points and sixteen rounds. Each kill earns four rounds, up to twenty-four. Watch for larger boss ships as the fight goes on.
