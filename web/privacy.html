Privacy

Your high score

The game saves your best score locally as JSON. The browser edition uses your browser’s local storage. The desktop edition writes a file called highscore.json in the game folder.

Your score stays on the device and browser profile where you played. Clearing site data removes your browser record. If your browser blocks storage, you can still play and see your best score for that session.

Hosting

When you play on itch.io, your browser loads the game and its artwork and sounds from itch.io.
